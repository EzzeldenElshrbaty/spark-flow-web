Chat holds the workspace’s channels, every project’s channel and your direct messages.

![A channel](chat.jpg "Messages, the composer and the channel’s tabs")

## Channels and direct messages

Channels are listed in the menu on the Chat side; star one to keep it at the top, and drag to reorder. A project’s channel holds everyone on the project; every project’s channel is under **Project channels**, in the order of the Projects side. **New message** starts a direct message with one or more people.

A channel’s name at the top opens its details: topic, people, and who can post. People who can change any public channel can archive one; a project’s channel follows its project.

## Writing

`Enter` sends, `Shift` + `Enter` starts a new line. **Aa** shows the formatting. Drop or paste files to attach them. A link gets a preview card; the × on the card takes it off.

Write `@` to mention someone (they’re told), `@NEX-101` for a task, `@NEX` for a project, or `@all` for everyone in the channel.

## Shortcuts

Write `/` at the start of a message for shortcuts:

- `/remind` sets a reminder; `/task Fix login @Nour` creates a task with that title and assignee: in a project’s channel, in that project; anywhere else, in the project you pick first in the task sheet, and the channel gets your “Created SEL-103” message with the task’s card.
- `/invite` adds people; `/topic` sets the channel’s topic.
- `/mute` and `/leave` act on the channel; `/search` searches it.

## The message menu

Point at a message (or tap it on a phone) for reactions, **Reply in thread** and the ⋯ menu:

- **Get notified about replies**, **Mark unread** (`U`) and **Remind me about this**.
- **Create task**, **Copy link** and **Copy text**.
- **Edit** and **Delete** for your own messages. A deleted message can be brought back with **Undo** for a few seconds.

## The channel’s tabs and header

Under the channel’s name, **Messages**, **Files** (in folders if you like), **Pins** and **Page**; **+** adds a bookmark. In the header, the bell sets how much of the channel reaches you, 🔍 searches it, and ⋯ holds the rest.

## The channel’s page

**Page** is one shared page for the channel: plans, checklists, tables and links to tasks. Whoever can post in the channel can edit it, one person at a time. While someone types, everyone else reads it as it changes and sees who is editing. It saves a second after you stop typing, and the bar above it says when it was last saved and by whom.

- **Formatting:** the row above the page sets headings, lists, bold and italic, and adds a checklist or a table. `#`, `##`, `###`, `-`, `1.` and `[ ]` at the start of a line work too.
- **Mentions and tasks:** @ names someone, who hears about it in Activity. **Link a task** puts a task on the page.
- **If someone saved first:** your text stays where it is. **Copy my changes** keeps it, and **Load the latest** shows the page as it is now.
- **History** lists every version. Pick one to read it, and **Restore this version** saves it as the newest; nothing newer is lost.
- **Copy link** copies the page’s address.
