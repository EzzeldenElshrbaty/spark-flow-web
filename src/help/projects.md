Projects lists every project in the workspace. The cards at the top count the active ones, the overdue ones, those due this month and those on hold.

![The Projects list](projects.jpg "Each row shows the phase, progress and hours")

## The list

Each row shows the client, the project manager, the current phase, the due date, progress, hours logged and how many tasks are overdue. People who can see cost also see each project's cost. Click a row to open the project.

A task is **overdue** when it's past its due date, or has more hours logged than estimated, and isn't done.

## Starting a project

**New project** needs "Create projects". Give it a name, a key of 2 to 5 capital letters (such as NEX), a manager, people and teams, and its dates. Every project has phases; agile ones also have sprints. The key starts every task's name (NEX-101) and can't be used again, not even after the project is deleted.

Each project gets its own chat channel, with its people in it. See [The project page](help:project-page).
