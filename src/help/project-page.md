A project's page has five tabs: Overview, Tasks, Chat, Time and History. The header shows the client, the manager, the dates and the project's links, and the phases run under it, the current one marked.

## Overview

What the project is about, its deliverables, its team and its files.

![A project's Overview tab](project-overview.jpg "Phases, deliverables, team and files")

- **Edit project** (its manager, and anyone who can change any project) changes the details, people and teams.
- **Manage phases**, in the ⋯ menu, adds, renames and dates phases.
- **Archive project**, in the ⋯ menu, asks why, then makes the project read-only and archives its channel. Timers running on it stop. People who can archive projects restore it here or from [Archived records](help:archived).

## Tasks

The board and list of the project's tasks. See [Board and tasks](help:tasks).

## Chat

The project's own channel, with everyone on the project. It works as it does in Chat: the same messages, composer, threads, files and pins. **Open in Chat** opens it there. A task comment or bug also posts a card here, so the team sees it.

## Time

Time logged on the project. See [Time](help:time).

## History

Every change to the project and its tasks: who changed what, and when.
