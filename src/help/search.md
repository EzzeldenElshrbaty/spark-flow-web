Search finds messages, files, channels and people you can see.

## Starting a search

- In the top bar’s box, choose its last row, or press `Ctrl` + `Enter`.
- In a channel, the 🔍 in its header or `Ctrl` + `F` searches that channel.
- In the composer, `/search` does the same.

## Narrowing it

The chips under the box narrow it, or type them yourself: `in:` a channel, `from:` a person, `before:`, `after:` and `on:` a date, `has:` (such as `has:file`) and `is:`. The search stays in the address, so you can share it or come back to it.

You only find what you could open: messages in channels you’re in, and public channels.
