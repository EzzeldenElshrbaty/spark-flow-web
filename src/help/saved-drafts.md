## Saved

**Save for later** on a message or file keeps it in Saved. **Remind me about this** saves it with a time; Activity tells you when it's due. **Mark complete** moves one to Completed when you've dealt with it.

## Drafts & sent

What you started writing and didn't send stays as a draft, in its channel and in Drafts & sent. A message can also be scheduled to send later (the arrow beside Send); until then it waits there, where you can change it, send it now or cancel it. What you sent lately is there too.
