A project's **Tasks** tab shows its tasks as a board or a list.

![A project's board](board.jpg "Drag a card to change its status")

## The board

Each column is a status, with the hours logged in it. Drag a card to another column to change its status, or within a column to change its order. The chips narrow it to a phase, and agile projects also pick a sprint. **Board** and **List** switch the view.

A card shows the task's key, priority, who it's assigned to and its hours against its estimate, in red once it's overdue.

**New task** (the project's manager and people who can run any project) adds one. Give it a title, and if you like a description, priority, story points, an estimate, a due date and who it's assigned to.

## The task panel

Click a task to open it at the side. You can change its fields if your role allows; the person it's assigned to can always move its status. **Start timer** logs time on it.

Below is its activity: comments and bugs, with files. Write `@` to mention someone, `@NEX-101` for a task, or `@NEX` for a project. A comment or bug also posts a card in the project's chat.

**Archive task** asks why, then hides it from the board. Its time stays in reports.

## My tasks

My tasks lists your open tasks across every project, overdue first.
