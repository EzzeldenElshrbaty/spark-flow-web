A project’s **Tasks** tab shows its tasks as a board or a list.

![A project’s board](board.jpg "Drag a card to change its status")

## The board

Each column is a status, with the hours logged in it. Drag a card to another column to change its status, or within a column to change its order. The chips narrow it to a phase, and agile projects also pick a sprint: the board and the list open on the running sprint until you pick another sprint or **All sprints**. **Board** and **List** switch the view, and agile projects add **Sprints**.

A card shows the task’s key, priority, who it’s assigned to and its hours against its estimate. A red **Overdue** chip means its due date has passed; an amber **Over estimate** chip, with its hours in amber, means more hours are logged than estimated. A task can have both.

**New task** (the project’s manager and people who can run any project) adds one. Give it a title, and if you like a description, priority, story points, an estimate, a due date and who it’s assigned to.

The description takes formatting: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, links, lists, quotes and code, from the toolbar, the usual keys (⌘/Ctrl B, I, U, K) or as you type (`*bold*`, `- ` for a list). **⌘/Ctrl+Enter** saves the task. Drop, paste or attach files into it: each uploads and shows where you put it, a picture as a picture. Every file in the description is also under **Attachments**, where you can add more (up to 20 a task). Taking a file off Attachments takes it out of the description too. **Save task** waits until the files finish uploading.

## Sprints

On an agile project, **Sprints** plans the work: the running sprint first, then each planned sprint, then **Backlog**, the unfinished tasks in no sprint. Each sprint shows its dates, the days left while it runs, its goal, and its tasks and points.

An Admin or the project’s manager runs the sprints; everyone on the project sees the same list.

- **Create sprint** adds the next one (Sprint 16 after Sprint 15), two weeks long, starting after the last.
- To plan a sprint, drag a task by its handle into it, or use the task’s **Move to** (the way on a phone). From the keyboard, Space on the handle picks a task up, the arrow keys move it, and Space puts it down.
- **Start sprint** confirms the name, goal and dates. It opens on today and keeps the length the sprint was planned for. One sprint runs at a time, and it needs a task to start.
- **Complete sprint** keeps the done tasks with it and moves the rest to the next planned sprint, another planned one, a new sprint or the backlog.
- **⋯** edits a sprint. A planned sprint can also be deleted there; its tasks go to the backlog.

Starting and completing a sprint each post a card in the project’s chat. A completed sprint leaves this list; pick it in the board’s sprint filter to see its tasks.

## The task panel

Click a task to open it at the side. You can change its fields if your role allows; the person it’s assigned to can always move its status. **Start timer** logs time on it. **Expand panel** makes the panel wider (the whole screen on a phone), and it opens that way next time; **Shrink panel** turns it back. **Open in project** takes you to the task on its project’s board, with the panel still open.

Its description shows as written, with its files: click one to open it, and **Download** is in the viewer. **Attachments · N** lists the task’s files. Files are added and removed in **Edit task**.

Its fields name the project, and on agile projects the sprint and points; **Made from** links to the chat message a task was made from. Below, in the same scroll, are its **Comments**: comments and bugs, with files. Write `@` to mention someone, `@NEX-101` for a task, or `@NEX` for a project. A comment or bug also posts a card in the project’s chat. Last is its **History**: every change, newest first.

**Archive task** asks why, then hides it from the board. Its time stays in reports.
