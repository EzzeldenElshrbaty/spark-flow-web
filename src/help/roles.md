Each role is a set of permissions; a person holds one or more roles and may do everything any of them allows.

![Roles and permissions](roles.jpg "Permissions down the side, roles across")

## Changing what a role may do

Tick or untick a permission under a role. Changes wait until **Save changes**, so you can change several at once; the count shows how many are waiting.

Some rights come from the record, whatever the role: a project’s manager runs that project, and you can edit your own messages.

## Roles of your own

**Add role** starts empty or as a copy of another. A role’s ⋯ menu renames or deletes it; only a role nobody holds can be deleted. Admin, Sales, Project manager and Member are built in: they can be changed but not deleted.
