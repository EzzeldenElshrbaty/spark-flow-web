Preferences (in your menu) decide what reaches you.

## Notifications

Choose **All new messages**, **Mentions and keywords**, or **Nothing**. A channel can have its own level from its bell; **Default** there goes back to following this one.

**Keywords** count as mentions: a message with one of them reaches you as if it named you.

## Do Not Disturb

Pauses notifications for 30 minutes, 1 or 2 hours, or until tomorrow at 9:00 AM. A moon on your picture shows it’s on.

## Browser notifications

With Flow not in front, your browser can show new mentions and direct messages. Your browser asks you to allow it first.
