Users lists everyone in the company, active or not, with their roles, teams and when they were last active. Search, or narrow by role, team and status.

![Settings, Users](users.jpg "Everyone, with roles, teams and status")

## Adding and editing

**Add person** takes a name, an email (each person has their own), a title, one or more roles and their teams. Click someone to edit them the same way. A person gets every permission of every role they hold; see [Roles and permissions](help:roles).

## Deactivating

**Deactivate**, in someone’s sheet, signs them out of Flow, stops their timer and takes them out of every list you pick people from. What they did stays, with their name on it. You can’t deactivate yourself, or the last person who can change settings, users and roles.

**Reactivate** brings them back.

> Sign-in and invitations arrive with the backend. In the prototype, try a new person with **View as** in your menu.
