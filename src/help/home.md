My work is your day in one place: your timer and today’s time, every open task assigned to you, and the latest mentions of you. It’s in the sidebar under Work, after the [Dashboard](help:dashboard) you start on; on a phone, it’s in **More**.

![My work with a running timer](home.jpg "Today, with the timer on a task")

## Today

Starting a timer starts your day: there’s nothing else to do first. **Start timer** lists your open tasks first, then the ones you timed lately; type to find any open task on your projects, and pick one to start. **Log on the project only** times the whole project. The top bar has the same button on every page.

The first time, the dialog says what a timer start records: the time, this device’s IP address and browser, and your location if your browser allows it. You, the managers of your projects and people who can see everyone’s timers see them in [Attendance](help:attendance).

## The timer

One timer runs at a time: starting another stops the last.

- **Pause** stops the count and keeps the task ready. **Resume** carries the count on from where it stopped.
- **Stop** ends it. The next start counts from zero.
- **Switch task**, while paused, starts the timer on another task.
- A paused timer clears at the end of the day.

The timer also shows in the top bar (and above the bottom bar on phones), so you can pause or stop it from any page. **Add time** logs time nobody timed, for an Admin or a project’s manager. See [Time](help:time) for the rules.

## My tasks

Every open task assigned to you, across every project: overdue first, then the ones over their estimate, then by due date. Open one to see its panel, or press **Start** to start your timer on it. The old My tasks address opens this page.

## Mentions

When someone writes `@` and your name in a message or comment, it shows here and in the bell. The bell also tells you when someone assigns you a task, moves a task of yours or comments on one. Open it to go to the message. **See all** opens [Activity](help:threads-activity).
