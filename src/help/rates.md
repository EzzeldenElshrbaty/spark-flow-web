Hourly cost sets what each person costs per hour, in EGP. Project cost and [Time and cost](help:time-cost) multiply hours by the rate that applied when the time was logged, so changing a rate doesn't change the past.

Only people who can see and change hourly costs open this page. Cost itself shows to people who can see project cost.
