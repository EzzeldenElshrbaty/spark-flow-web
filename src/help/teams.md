Teams lists the workspace's teams and their people. Putting a team on a project brings all its people onto it: they see the project, get its tasks and join its chat.

A person's teams are set on [Users](help:users).

## Making and changing teams

People who can change settings (Admins, by default) see **New team** at the top and a **⋯** on each team with **Rename** and **Remove**. A name is up to 40 characters and can't be another team's in the workspace.

A team a project still uses can't be removed: take it off the project first (Edit project). Removing a team takes it off its people; they stay in the workspace.
