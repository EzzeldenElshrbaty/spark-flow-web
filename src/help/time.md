Hours come from the timer and from time added by hand. A task’s hours count against its estimate.

## The timer

Press **Start timer** in the top bar and pick a task: yours come first, then recent ones, and typing finds any open task on your projects. Picking one starts the timer; the very first time, the dialog says what a timer records and **Start timer** confirms. You can also start it from My work or a task’s panel. One timer runs at a time: starting another stops the last.

- **Pause** stops counting and keeps the task in the bar. **Resume** carries the count on from where it stopped.
- **Stop** ends it, and the bar shows Start timer again. The next start counts from zero.
- **Switch task** on a paused timer starts another task instead.
- A paused timer clears at the end of the day.
- A timer that runs as long as the timer timeout (4 hours unless your company sets it otherwise) pauses and asks **Still working?**

## Adding time

**Add time** logs time nobody timed. Only people who can see and change everyone’s time, and a project’s manager on that project, add time; everyone else logs their time with the timer. Pick a task or a whole project, whose time it is under **Person**, the start and end, and a note if you like. An entry can’t overlap another of that person’s, end in the future or run over 12 hours.

You can change or delete your own entries once they’ve stopped. People who can see and change everyone’s time can change anyone’s.

## Adding time for someone else

After the project and task, pick whose time it is under **Person**, every time: the list has **Me**, then the project’s people. The same rules apply as if they had added it themselves, so it can’t overlap their other entries.

The entry is theirs, and says who added it ("Added by Nour El-Sayed"). Whoever added it can also change or delete it.

## A project’s Time tab

The project’s entries, newest first. You see your own; the project’s manager and people who can see everyone’s time see everyone’s. People who can see cost also see what each entry cost.
