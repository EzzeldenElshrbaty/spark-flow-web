A thread is a message with its replies. Replying in a thread keeps the channel tidy; **Also send to #channel** posts your reply in the channel too.

## Threads

Threads (in the Chat side's menu, or `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `T` where the browser lets it through) lists the threads you follow, newest reply first, with what's new in each. You follow a thread when you wrote its first message, replied, or were mentioned in it.

To follow one yourself, choose **Get notified about replies** in the message's ⋯ menu or the thread panel's. **Unfollow** on a card stops it.

## Muting a thread

**Mute thread**, in the thread panel's ⋮ or a card's ⋯, keeps following the thread but stops its replies from counting as new or reaching Activity, the bell and desktop notifications. A reply that mentions you still reaches you. The card shows **Muted**; **Unmute thread** undoes it. **Open in a new window**, in the thread panel's ⋮, opens the thread in its own tab.

## Activity

Activity (in the Chat side's menu, or `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `A`) lists what happened to you: mentions, task news (someone assigned you a task, moved a task of yours, or commented on one; the **Tasks** tab), replies in threads you follow, reactions to your messages and reminders. Opening an item reads it; **Mark all read** reads them all.
