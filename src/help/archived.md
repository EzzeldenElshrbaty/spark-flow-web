Every archived project, task and channel in one list, with when, by whom and why. Search names, keys and reasons, or narrow by kind.

![Archived records](archived.jpg "Each with when it can be deleted for good")

## Restoring

**Restore** brings a record back, with the rights its own page needs. A task in an archived project says to restore the project first.

## Deleting for good

Records are kept for a number of days after archiving (365 unless changed with **Change**); after that, **Delete…** removes one for good, with its tasks, comments, phases and files. It asks you to type the record’s key (such as `NEX` or `NEX-101`) first, and can’t be undone; the audit log keeps a line saying so.

Some are always kept:

- anything with time logged on it, so its hours and cost stay in reports;
- channels, which are restored only.

A deleted project’s key is never used again.
