Attendance shows check-ins and time using Flow. Everyone sees their own (**My attendance** in your menu). People who can see everyone's check-ins see everyone; a project's manager sees the people on their projects.

![Attendance, Check-ins](attendance.jpg "A new IP or device is marked in amber")

## What's recorded

Each check-in and check-out records the time, the IP address and browser, and the location if the browser allows it ("Location blocked" when it doesn't, "No location" when the device has none). The location opens in Google Maps.

While Flow is on screen and you used it in the last 5 minutes, it counts a minute of app time each minute. A tab in the background, or a computer left on, doesn't count.

> In the prototype the IP address is made up for each browser: a browser can't see its own. The real server records the real one.

## The views

- **Check-ins**: each check-in and check-out. An IP or device that differs from that person's last one is marked in amber with "new".
- **Day view**: one day, a row per person: in, out, hours checked in and app time.
- **Activity log**: each stretch of time using Flow.
- **Report**: per person, days in, average in and out, late arrivals, early leaves, hours and app time.

Pick a person and a period (today, 7, 30 or 90 days) at the top.

## Working hours

Late is in after the start of a working day; left early is out before its end. A check-out Flow made itself (a forgotten one) is marked "By Flow" and never counts as early. People who can change settings set the working days and hours with **Change**.
