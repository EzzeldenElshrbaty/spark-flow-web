The Dashboard shows the projects you look after. People who can see the dashboard for every project see them all; a project's manager sees the ones they manage.

![The Dashboard](dashboard.jpg "Every project at a glance, and who is working now")

## The projects

Each project shows its phase, progress, hours against its estimate, the expected finish date and who worked on it this week. People who can see cost also see each project's cost.

## Working now

Who has a timer running, since when, and what it's on. People who can see everyone's timers see every one; a project's manager sees the timers on the projects they manage. A paused timer isn't listed. More is in [Attendance](help:attendance).
