Spark Flow runs Spark Systems' projects and its chat in one place. This chapter covers what’s around every page.

## Signing in

Sign in with your Spark email and password. You start on your [Dashboard](help:dashboard). Signed out, any link opens Sign in first, then the page it pointed to (the workspace’s own address opens the Dashboard). **Log out** is in your menu.

> In the prototype, everyone’s password is `flow2026`, and the list under the form signs you in as anyone, by role, to try what each role can do.

![The sign-in page](signin.jpg "Email and password, and the prototype’s people by role")

## The two sides

The menu on the left shows one side at a time: **Projects** (your day, projects, tasks, time) or **Chat** (channels, messages and threads). Switch with the toggle under the logo, or press `G` then `P` for Projects and `G` then `C` for Chat. Each side reopens where you left it; the first time, Projects opens on the Dashboard and Chat on #general.

## Search

The box at the top finds projects, tasks, people and pages as you type. Press `Ctrl` + `K` (`⌘` + `K` on a Mac) to jump to it, unless you’re writing a message, where it adds a link. Its last row searches messages and files; see [Search](help:search).

## Help

The round **?** beside the bell opens help for the page you’re on. It stays open as you move between pages, so you can read and work at once. At its foot, **Open the help centre** shows every chapter, with search.

## Your menu

Your name at the top right opens:

- **Preferences**: notifications, keywords and Do Not Disturb. See [Preferences](help:preferences).
- **My attendance**: your days from your timers, and time in Flow. See [Attendance](help:attendance).
- **Help**: the help centre.
- **Light** or **Dark**: how Flow looks on this device.

> In the prototype, **View as** lets you try Flow as someone else, to see what their roles allow.

## Lists and tables

Drag a column’s edge to make it wider; double-click to reset it. Click a header to sort. Filters and searches stay in the address, so a link you share opens the same view.

## Unsaved changes

A form you’ve started doesn’t lose what you typed by accident. A click outside it never closes it, and neither does **Esc** on a page’s forms (in chat’s dialogs, **Esc** works like Cancel). **Cancel**, **✕** or your browser’s Back ask “Discard your changes?” first: **Keep editing** takes you back to the form, and **Discard** closes it without saving. A form you haven’t changed closes straight away.

## On a phone

The bar at the bottom holds Dashboard, Projects, Chat and More; More holds My work, Time & cost, Teams, Settings and Help. Tables become lists, and sheets fill the screen. Everything else works the same.
