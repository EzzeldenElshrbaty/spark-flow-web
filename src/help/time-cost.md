Time and cost adds up the hours everyone logged, and what they cost.

![Time and cost](time-cost.jpg "Hours and cost for the last 30 days")

## Reading it

Pick **7**, **30** or **90** days, ending now, and one project or all of them. Totals come first, then **By project**, **By person** and **By week** (weeks start on Sunday). Cost is hours times each person's [hourly cost](help:rates) at the time.

## Downloading

**Export CSV** saves what's on screen as a spreadsheet. The download is noted in the [audit log](help:audit-log).
